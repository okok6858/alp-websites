Solutions

Our portfolio covers vehicle and equipment simulators designed for reliable daily operation in arcades, training centres, trade shows, and dedicated entertainment venues. Each system combines realistic controls, responsive software, and durable hardware suited to both B2B operators and private buyers.

01

Rally & motorsport simulators

High-engagement driving rigs with force feedback, multi-screen or motion options, and software tuned for competitive and casual play. Ideal for entertainment centres, events, and branded experiential spaces.

02

Forestry machinery simulators

Interactive training and demonstration systems that replicate heavy equipment operation. Used by educators, employers, and exhibitors who need safe, repeatable practice without putting real machinery at risk.

03

Bus & commercial vehicle simulators

Cab layouts, pedals, and steering interfaces matched to public transport and fleet contexts. Supports driver familiarisation, assessment programmes, and public-facing showcase installations.

04

Custom vehicle & equipment rigs

Bespoke simulation builds for specialised vehicles, industrial equipment, or themed attractions. We align hardware, software, and enclosure design with your floor plan, audience, and operating model.

05

Interactive entertainment systems

Beyond single-seat rigs: multi-user setups, linked leaderboards, and integrated control for attraction operators who want a cohesive guest journey from ticket to session.

06

Supply, setup & support

Equipment sales with guidance on placement, power, and maintenance. We help you choose configurations that match throughput, skill level, and whether the primary goal is play, instruction, or demonstration.

Applications

Recreation

Arcade floors, family entertainment centres, and hospitality venues that need reliable, crowd-pleasing experiences with straightforward operator workflows.

Education

Schools, colleges, and vocational programmes that use simulators to teach vehicle dynamics, safety awareness, and equipment handling in a controlled environment.

Training

Employers and training providers who want repeatable scenarios, progress tracking, and lower risk before learners move to live equipment or public roads.

Exhibition

Trade fairs, product launches, and brand activations where a hands-on simulator draws visitors and communicates capability more effectively than static displays alone.

About
